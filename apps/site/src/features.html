Features

Everything you need. Nothing it can see.

The parts of a password manager you reach for every day, each one built so that the server never learns what you store.

Security you do not have to think about

Encrypted on your device

AES-256-GCM with a fresh nonce for every write. Keys are derived in the browser and never cross the network.

Names and tags too

Not just passwords. Entry names, usernames, websites, notes and tags are all encrypted, so a stolen database does not even list which sites you use.

Two-factor codes built in

Store authenticator keys alongside the login they belong to and get live one-time codes, generated locally to the RFC 6238 standard.

Strong passwords, generated

A generator drawing from the browser's cryptographic random source, with a strength meter on every password you save.

Copy that cleans up

Copy a username, password or code in one click. Secrets are cleared from the clipboard after thirty seconds.

Locks itself

Keys live in memory only — never in browser storage or cookies. Close the tab, reload, or step away for fifteen minutes and the vault is locked.

Organised

Find anything in a keystroke

Your whole vault is decrypted in memory once you unlock it, so search and filtering are instant — and happen entirely on your device. The server could not search your vault if it tried.

  • Seven entry types — logins, secure notes, cards, identities, bank accounts, SSH keys and Wi-Fi
  • Instant search across names, usernames, websites and tags. Press / from anywhere
  • Favourites and tags for the things you open most
  • Filters by type with live counts
The details

Small things, done properly

A generator you can trust

Passwords drawn from the browser's cryptographic random source with no bias between characters. Choose the length and character sets.

Strength at a glance

Every saved password shows a strength meter, so weak ones stand out when you open them.

No icon lookups

Entries get a coloured initial instead of a website icon. Fetching icons would tell a third party which sites you use.

Light and dark

Follows your system theme, with a layout designed for both rather than one inverted from the other.

Works on any screen

Three panes on a desktop, two on a tablet, one on a phone. Nothing to install — it runs in the browser.

Locks itself

Keys never touch browser storage. Reloading, closing the tab, or fifteen idle minutes locks the vault.

Your secrets, readable only by you.

Open your vault in the browser. Nothing to install.
